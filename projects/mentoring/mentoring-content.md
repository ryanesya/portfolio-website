### 4. Why I mentor
When I was a fresh graduate, I struggled too. I had no idea what to do to land my first job. I went to
every workshop and sharing session I could find and taught myself product design from scratch, back when
structured bootcamps like today's didn't exist yet. I know first-hand how hard it is to break into this
field, especially product design. That is what makes me feel called to share what I know, and what I have
lived through, with young talent and people looking to switch careers.

Main goal → Help aspiring designers understand product design and land the roles they are aiming for,
the way I wish someone had helped me when I was starting out.
